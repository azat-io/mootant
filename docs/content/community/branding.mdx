Writing about Mootant? Here are the logos, colors and typefaces to make it feel
at home in your article, presentation or project.

<BrandIntro />

## Logo

A cow with a little mutation: one extra eye. Use the standalone mark when space
is limited or the Mootant name already appears nearby.

<BrandLogos kind="logo" />

## Wordmark

The name and its green third eye. Use the wordmark when introducing Mootant or
when the name needs to stand on its own.

<BrandLogos kind="wordmark" />

Both marks are available as SVGs with transparent backgrounds. Choose the
version for your background; the artwork scales without losing detail.

## Colors

Bright green brings the mutation. Graphite and off-white give it room. These are
the three core colors used in the logos and on this site.

<BrandPalette />

On light backgrounds, the site uses a darker green for text and links to keep
them readable. The bright green is for accents, logos and filled surfaces.

## Typography

**Sora** gives the site's headings and body text their shape. **JetBrains Mono**
is used for code, commands and technical labels. The wordmark is supplied as
vector artwork, so it does not need a font installed.

<BrandTypography />

## Usage

- Keep the original proportions and colors of the artwork.
- Give the mark some space, away from text, borders and other logos.
- Use the dark artwork on light backgrounds and the light artwork on dark
  backgrounds.
- Place the logo on a clear, uncluttered surface so the extra eye stays visible.

In writing, use **Mootant** for the project name and `mootant` for the package
and command.
